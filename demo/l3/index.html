The flash-sale overlay, the countdown, the rotating deals, and the autoplaying hero are here to overload the page on purpose. Lift’s Layer 3 watches only on-device signals: erratic mouse jitter (path length over displacement plus direction-change rate), rage clicks (three or more clicks in 700 ms inside a 30 px radius), rapid scroll reversals, and the simulated biometric slider. Those samples are scored in a 5 second window with weights 0.30 / 0.25 / 0.25 / 0.20 and then forgotten.

When the combined score stays at or above 0.62 for 1.5 seconds, the runtime calls Flagsmith’s JS SDK setTrait('overstimulated', true) on the sticky lift_vid identity. Flagsmith evaluates the sensory_safe segment rule overstimulated == true and serves the l3_sensory_safe flag. The page reads that flag. It does not apply the mode from a local if (score). Returning to calm requires the score to stay below 0.38 for 8 seconds, plus a cooldown, and the restore fades over about 2 seconds.

Returning visitors keep the same identity via the lift_vid cookie or a ?lift_identity=lift-<uuid> value. Checkout is marked data-lift-essential so Sensory-Safe never hides it, never strips focus, and never changes its role or accessible name. Only data-lift-promo and data-lift-marketing hide.
